## What is cold water therapy?
Cold water therapy (CWT) includes ice baths or cold plunges to reduce soreness and aid recovery perception. It can help acute relief but may blunt hypertrophy signaling if overused immediately post-lift.

:::takeaway
Use CWT **away from hypertrophy sessions** or on rest days; limit to **5-15 minutes** to avoid dampening muscle gains.
:::

## How does it work?
Cold reduces tissue temperature and may dampen inflammation and nerve conduction, decreasing soreness. Post-lift cold can reduce muscle protein synthesis signals, potentially slowing [[hypertrophy]] if used chronically right after training. It may aid adherence by improving how you feel.

## Practical application
If you plunge, do it on rest days or several hours after lifting. Keep sessions 5-15 minutes at tolerable temps. Rewarm gradually. Do not force through numbness or pain. Prioritize [[sleep]], protein, and programmed deloads for core recovery; treat CWT as optional.

:::checklist
- Timing | Use on rest days or hours after lifting, not immediately post-hypertrophy.
- Duration | **5-15 min**; stop if overly uncomfortable.
- Safety | Rewarm slowly; avoid if you have cold-related conditions.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Jumping in right after lifting and potentially blunting growth signals.
- Staying too long or too cold, risking faintness or numbness.
- Using cold to mask poor programming or lack of sleep.
