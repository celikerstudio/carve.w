## What is citrulline?
Citrulline (often as citrulline malate) supports nitric oxide production, potentially improving blood flow, pumps, and performance in repeated efforts.

:::takeaway
Take **4-8 g L-citrulline** (or **6-8 g malate**) **30-60 min pre** to support pumps and repeated effort quality.
:::

## How does it work?
Citrulline converts to arginine, raising nitric oxide. Better blood flow may enhance nutrient delivery and reduce perceived fatigue in multi-set work. Effects are modest but notable for some lifters, especially in high-[[volume|volume]] sessions.

## Practical application
Dose 4-8 g L-citrulline (or 6-8 g malate) pre-workout with water. Pair with carbs and hydration. Avoid under-dosed proprietary blends. If GI upset occurs, start at 3-4 g and build. Works well in pump-focused or accessory blocks; less critical for low-rep heavy days.

:::checklist
- Dose | **4-8 g** L-citrulline or **6-8 g** malate.
- Timing | **30-60 min** pre-lift.
- Pairing | Combine with carbs/hydration; verify full label dosing.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Using low-dose blends that miss effective ranges.
- Expecting big strength boosts on 1RM work.
- Ignoring hydration, which limits pump and performance benefits.
