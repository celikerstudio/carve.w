## What are micronutrients?
Micronutrients are vitamins and minerals required for metabolism, immunity, and recovery. They support energy production, bone health, and nervous system function.

:::takeaway
Prioritize varied whole foods: fruits, vegetables, whole grains, dairy/fortified options, lean proteins, and some nuts/seeds to cover micronutrient needs.
:::

## How does it work?
Micronutrients act as cofactors in energy pathways, muscle contraction, and tissue repair. Deficiencies can blunt performance, recovery, and [[sleep]]. Higher training loads may increase needs for some minerals (e.g., iron in endurance, magnesium for muscle/nerve function).

## Practical application
Eat 2-3 fruits and 3-5 vegetable servings daily; include dairy or fortified alternatives for calcium; use whole grains and legumes for B vitamins and minerals; add nuts/seeds for magnesium and healthy [[fats]]. If diet is restricted, consider targeted supplements (e.g., vitamin D, iron under guidance). Rotate colors of produce to broaden coverage. Avoid megadoses unless prescribed.

:::checklist
- Produce | **5-8 servings/day** across colors for broad coverage.
- Foundations | Include calcium sources; whole grains/legumes for B-vitamins and minerals.
- Gaps | Use bloodwork to guide targeted supplements; avoid shotgun megadoses.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Over-relying on a multivitamin instead of improving diet quality.
- Low produce intake, leading to fiber and micronutrient gaps.
- Ignoring lab-confirmed deficiencies like vitamin D or iron.
