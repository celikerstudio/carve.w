## What is a deload?
A deload is a planned reduction in training stress—usually volume, sometimes intensity—to dissipate fatigue and restore performance. It keeps long blocks of training productive and reduces injury risk.

:::takeaway
Every **4-10 weeks**, drop **30-50% volume** and slightly lower [[intensity]] to reset fatigue while maintaining skill.
:::

## How does it work?
Accumulated fatigue from high [[volume]], [[intensity]], and life stress can mask gains. A deload reduces workload, allowing recovery systems to catch up. Maintaining some intensity preserves movement skill and neural adaptations without adding much fatigue.
Deloads also resensitize you to training stress, so the following block drives adaptation again. Short-term performance dips often rebound after just a few lighter sessions when sleep and nutrition are aligned.

## Practical application
Schedule deloads after hard blocks or when readiness dips (persistent soreness, slower bar speed, poor mood). Cut sets 30-50%, keep 1-2 lighter top sets at ~80-85% of recent loads, and hold RPE around 6-7. Keep technique crisp and sessions short. Resume normal training once performance and energy rebound.
You can deload by exercise, day, or whole week depending on fatigue location. Keep movement patterns similar so skill holds, but trim extras that pile fatigue (long drop sets, high accessories). Use the week to clean up technique and dial in sleep and food.
If life stress is high, extend the deload by a few extra easy sessions and prioritize low-intensity movement and hydration. Log how you feel so future deload timing improves.

:::checklist
- Timing | Insert every **4-10 weeks** or at clear fatigue signals.
- Volume cut | Reduce sets **30-50%**; keep some intensity at lower RPE.
- Exit criteria | Return when bar speed, energy, and mood improve across sessions.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Skipping deloads until forced by pain or stagnation.
- Dropping intensity to zero, losing skill practice.
- Cutting volume too little, failing to reduce fatigue meaningfully.
