## What is travel and fitness?
Travel and fitness is maintaining training and nutrition while on the road. It emphasizes flexibility and planning to keep momentum.

:::takeaway
Pack **bands/mini-program**, prioritize **protein each meal**, and schedule **short sessions** (20-40 min) around travel days.
:::

## How does it work?
Portable workouts and nutrition anchors reduce disruption. Even reduced [[volume]] maintains skill and strength better than full breaks. Hotel gyms/bodyweight work plus protein-forward eating minimize regressions. Sleep and hydration offset travel fatigue.

## Practical application
Carry bands and a simple plan (push/pull/legs bodyweight + bands). Book hotels with a gym if possible. Train earlier in the day before work events. Build plates around protein (eggs, yogurt, lean meats), add fruit/veggies, and moderate rich add-ons. Hydrate, especially on flights; manage time zones with light exposure and consistent bed/wake times.

:::checklist
- Plan | Bands + 2-3 short routines; check hotel gym.
- Food | Protein at each meal; add fruit/veg; watch liquid calories/alcohol.
- Recovery | Hydrate, walk after flights, align light/sleep to local time.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Dropping all training instead of scaling to shorter sessions.
- Overeating restaurant meals without protein anchors.
- Ignoring sleep/jet lag management, extending fatigue.
