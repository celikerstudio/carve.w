## What is collagen?
Collagen supplements provide amino acids (including glycine/proline) that support connective tissue. They are not a complete protein for muscle growth but may aid joints/tendons alongside protein and rehab.

:::takeaway
Take **10-15 g** collagen with **50-100 mg vitamin C** **30-60 min pre-rehab** for tendons/ligaments; use complete proteins for muscle.
:::

## How does it work?
Collagen provides building blocks for connective tissue. Small studies show combining collagen + vitamin C before loading may support tendon/ligament health. It lacks enough essential amino acids for primary muscle protein synthesis compared with [[whey-protein]] or food.

## Practical application
Use 10-15 g collagen peptides with vitamin C 30-60 minutes before targeted rehab or high-tendon work (e.g., jumping). Count collagen toward total calories but not fully toward protein macros. Keep overall protein intake high with complete sources. Choose third-party tested products.

:::checklist
- Dose | **10-15 g** with **50-100 mg vitamin C** pre-rehab/loading.
- Purpose | Tendon/ligament support; not a primary muscle protein.
- Quality | Pick tested products; pair with proper loading programs.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Replacing complete proteins with collagen, shorting amino acids.
- Taking collagen without any loading stimulus and expecting tendon change.
- Skipping vitamin C co-ingestion when targeting connective tissue.
