## What is mobility?
Mobility is usable range of motion plus control. It supports technique, joint health, and performance in strength and [[hypertrophy]] training.

:::takeaway
Train strength through **full ROM**, add **targeted mobility** for sticky areas, and pair with control/tempo work.
:::

## How does it work?
Strength in long ranges, dynamic prep, and occasional static work expand and maintain ROM. Controlled eccentrics and pauses build stability in new ranges. Adequate load management prevents protective stiffness from overuse.

## Practical application
Identify limiting joints (hips, ankles, thoracic spine). Combine 2-3 mobility drills with strength in that range (e.g., split squats for hips/ankles). Use pauses and controlled eccentrics in main lifts. Brief static stretching post-lift can help maintain gains. If pain persists, reduce load and address technique.

:::checklist
- Assess | Target specific limits (ankle dorsiflexion, hip flexion, T-spine).
- Integrate | Pair mobility drills with strength in the new range (pauses/tempos).
- Consistency | Small daily doses beat marathon sessions.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Only stretching without loading the new range.
- Random drills not tied to the lifts that need better ROM.
- Pushing into pain instead of adjusting load/technique.
