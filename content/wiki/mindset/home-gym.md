## What is a home gym?
A home gym is a personal training space with selected equipment to support your goals. It removes commute friction and can boost [[consistency]].

:::takeaway
Start with **barbell or adjustable dumbbells**, **bench**, **rack**, and bands; expand slowly based on needs.
:::

## How does it work?
Convenience reduces skipped sessions. Equipment choices shape programming—versatile tools like dumbbells, kettlebells, and racks cover most needs. Environment and scheduling still matter for adherence.

## Practical application
Begin with essentials: adjustable DBs or barbell + plates, bench, rack/safeties, bands. Add a pull-up bar and some mats. Program around available tools (e.g., DB presses/rows, goblet/front squats). Set a fixed schedule and keep the space tidy. Add specialty items only when a need is clear.

:::checklist
- Essentials | DBs/barbell, bench, rack, bands, pull-up bar.
- Programming | Align lifts to tools; use safeties for heavy work.
- Habit | Fixed training times; keep the space organized.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Overbuying niche gear before using basics.
- Skipping safeties/anchors for heavy lifts.
- Letting the space get cluttered, raising friction to train.
