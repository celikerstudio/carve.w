## What is stretching?
Stretching improves flexibility and can help maintain range of motion. Its role is best as a post-training or separate session activity rather than heavy pre-lift static holds.

:::takeaway
Use **5-10 minutes** of light dynamic work pre-lift; save longer static stretching **post-lift or separate** to maintain mobility.
:::

## How does it work?
Dynamic stretching increases temperature and range safely before lifting. Static stretching can increase flexibility over time but may temporarily reduce force if done right before max efforts. Applied after training or separately, it helps keep joints moving well.

## Practical application
Pre-lift: dynamic drills (leg swings, arm circles) 5-10 minutes. Post-lift or off-days: 30-60s static holds for tight areas, 2-4 sets. Pair with strength through full ROM to make flexibility usable. Avoid painful ranges; progress gradually.

:::checklist
- Pre-lift | Dynamic moves **5-10 min** to prime motion.
- Post/Off | Static holds **30-60s**, 2-4 sets for target muscles.
- Combine | Train strength in full ROM to “own” new range.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Long static holds before heavy lifting, reducing power.
- Forcing painful ranges, causing irritation.
- Stretching without strengthening, making gains hard to use.
