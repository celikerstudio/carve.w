## What is gym etiquette?
Gym etiquette is respectful behavior that keeps the gym safe and comfortable. It supports focus and a positive training environment.

:::takeaway
Re-rack weights, wipe equipment, share space, and respect others’ focus and safety.
:::

## How does it work?
Clear norms prevent accidents, reduce conflict, and keep equipment available. Respecting space and hygiene maintains community goodwill and lets everyone train effectively.

## Practical application
Re-rack plates/dumbbells, wipe benches, don’t block mirrors/paths, and avoid filming others. Ask before working in; set timers and move promptly. Keep noise reasonable and follow gym rules. Offer spots only if asked/qualified.

:::checklist
- Cleanliness | Wipe equipment; use a towel.
- Courtesy | Re-rack, don’t hog stations; share on busy days.
- Safety | Don’t crowd lifters; ask before spotting/working in.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Leaving weights and sweat for others.
- Filming people without consent.
- Hogging equipment or ignoring queues.
