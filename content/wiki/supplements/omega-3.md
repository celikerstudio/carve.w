## What is omega-3?
Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) are essential fats found in fatty fish and some supplements. They support heart health, inflammation control, and may aid recovery and joint comfort for lifters.

:::takeaway
Common intake: **2-3 servings** of fatty fish weekly or **1-2 g/day combined EPA+DHA** via supplements taken with a fat-containing meal.
:::

## How does it work?
EPA and DHA incorporate into cell membranes, influencing inflammation and signaling. Better inflammation management can support joint comfort and recovery from [[volume]] and [[intensity]] training. Food sources also provide vitamin D and protein, complementing overall nutrition.
Omega-3s may modestly aid muscle protein synthesis signaling and endothelial function, which can support training quality indirectly. Consistent intake matters more than timing; effects accrue over weeks.

## Practical application
Eat salmon, mackerel, sardines, or trout 2-3 times weekly. If not, supplement 1-2 g combined EPA+DHA daily with meals containing [[fats]] for absorption. Choose products with third-party testing to avoid oxidation and contaminants. Store capsules cool and away from light. Track total fat and calories so fish oil doesn’t displace core diet targets.
If using fish oil, split doses with meals to reduce fish burps and improve tolerance. Check labels: algae oil can work for those avoiding fish, but match EPA/DHA amounts. Reevaluate intake if you already eat lots of fatty fish to avoid unnecessary high dosing.

:::checklist
- Intake | **2-3** fatty fish meals weekly or **1-2 g EPA+DHA** from supplements.
- Quality | Pick third-party tested oils; store cool and dark.
- Pairing | Take with meals containing fat to improve absorption.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Taking low-EPA/DHA doses that miss studied ranges.
- Storing capsules in heat/light, accelerating oxidation.
- Using omega-3 to cover for poor diet or lack of [[sleep]] and recovery habits.
