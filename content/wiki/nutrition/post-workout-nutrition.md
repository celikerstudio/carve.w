## What is post-workout nutrition?
Post-workout nutrition replenishes glycogen and supplies amino acids to repair muscle. It accelerates recovery after hard sessions.

:::takeaway
Within **0-3 hours**, eat **20-40 g protein** plus **20-80 g carbs**; larger carb doses after long/high-[[volume]] work.
:::

## How does it work?
Training depletes glycogen and triggers muscle protein breakdown. Protein provides amino acids for synthesis; carbs restore glycogen and reduce cortisol. Timing matters most when sessions are long, glycogen-depleting, or you train twice daily; otherwise the daily total is key.

## Practical application
Have a shake (20-30 g whey) with fruit/rice/oats, or a meal with lean protein and starch. Aim for 0.5-1.2 g/kg carbs if the session was long or another session follows. Pair with fluids/electrolytes as needed. Keep fat moderate to avoid slowing carb uptake when rapid refuel is desired.

:::checklist
- Protein | **20-40 g** high-quality source post-session.
- Carbs | **20-80 g**; higher after long/hard work or two-a-days.
- Fluids | Rehydrate; include sodium if sweating heavily.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Skipping carbs after long sessions, delaying recovery.
- Very high fat immediately post, slowing carb/protein uptake.
- Overemphasizing timing while neglecting daily protein and calories.
