## What is sauna?
Sauna is heat exposure used for relaxation, circulation, and potential cardiovascular benefits. It can support recovery perception when used wisely.

:::takeaway
Use **5-20 minutes** per session, 1-4x/week; hydrate and avoid immediately before intense training.
:::

## How does it work?
Heat elevates heart rate and circulation, inducing a light cardiovascular load and relaxation response. It may aid subjective recovery and stress reduction. Excessive heat without hydration can impair performance and [[sleep]] if used too close to bedtime.

## Practical application
Hydrate and include electrolytes if sweating heavily. Start with 5-10 minutes, build to 15-20 as tolerated. Use on rest days or after training, not right before heavy lifts. Cool down gradually. If dizzy or unwell, stop. Consult a clinician if you have cardiovascular conditions.

:::checklist
- Duration | **5-20 min**, 1-4x/week based on tolerance.
- Hydration | Replace fluids/electrolytes; avoid alcohol.
- Timing | Post-training or rest days; not before max efforts.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Overheating or staying too long, leading to dehydration.
- Using sauna right before heavy training, hurting performance.
- Ignoring health conditions or medications that affect heat tolerance.
