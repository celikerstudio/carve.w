## What is stress management?
Stress management uses habits and recovery practices to control mental and physical stress. Lower stress improves [[sleep]], training quality, and adherence to nutrition.

:::takeaway
Use daily stress hygiene: **sleep 7-9h**, short breath/walk breaks, consistent training load, and plan deloads when life stress is high.
:::

## How does it work?
Chronic stress elevates cortisol and sympathetic drive, impairing recovery, muscle gain, and fat loss. It can raise perceived effort and reduce motivation. Managing stress via sleep, movement, and routines lowers baseline arousal, improving readiness and decision-making.

## Practical application
Protect sleep with a wind-down routine. Insert short walks or breathing drills between work blocks. Keep training within recoverable [[volume]]; deload if life stress spikes. Eat regular meals with sufficient [[protein]] and carbs to stabilize energy. Limit excessive stimulants and late caffeine. Use simple mindfulness or journaling to downshift before bed.

:::checklist
- Sleep first | **7-9h** nightly with a consistent routine.
- Micro-breaks | Walks or 5-minute breathing between stress blocks.
- Load control | Adjust training/deload when stress rises; keep caffeine earlier in the day.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Adding more training when life stress is high, compounding fatigue.
- Relying on stimulants instead of fixing sleep and workload.
- Skipping meals and hydration during busy days, worsening stress response.
