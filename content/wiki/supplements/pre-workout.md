## What is pre-workout?
Pre-workout supplements combine stimulants, amino acids, and pumps/focus ingredients (e.g., caffeine, beta-alanine, citrulline) to enhance performance and perception of effort before training.

:::takeaway
Core stack: **3-6 mg/kg caffeine**, **4-6 g** L-citrulline (or 6-8 g citrulline malate), **2-3.2 g** beta-alanine daily; take **30-60 min** pre-lift.
:::

## How does it work?
Caffeine lowers perceived effort and boosts motor unit recruitment. Citrulline can increase nitric oxide and blood flow, while beta-alanine buffers acidity during higher-rep work (effects accrue with daily use). Synergistically, these can raise training quality (more reps, better bar speed) when dosing and timing are appropriate and [[sleep]] and nutrition are solid.
Other common ingredients: creatine (helps strength/size chronically, timing flexible), electrolytes for hydration, and tyrosine for focus—none replace sound programming or calories.

## Practical application
Start with caffeine at 3 mg/kg to assess tolerance; move up only if needed. Take citrulline 30-60 minutes pre; take beta-alanine daily (timing flexible) to build muscle carnosine. Stay hydrated and include sodium if training hot or long. Avoid high-caffeine doses late day to protect sleep. If sensitive, try lower-stim blends or stim-free (citrulline + electrolytes) and rely on carbs for energy.
Skip proprietary blends that hide doses; look for full labels that show clinically supported amounts. If heart rate or anxiety rise, dial back stimulants before adjusting other training variables.

:::checklist
- Dose | Caffeine **3-6 mg/kg**; citrulline **4-6 g** (or **6-8 g** malate); beta-alanine **2-3.2 g** daily.
- Timing | Take **30-60 min** pre-lift; beta-alanine timing is flexible but daily consistency matters.
- Safety | Track total caffeine from all sources; reduce or skip for evening sessions to protect sleep.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Mega-dosing stimulants, causing jitters and hurting sleep and recovery.
- Expecting beta-alanine to work immediately; it needs weeks of daily use.
- Using pre-workout to mask poor programming, low carbs, or lack of deloads.
