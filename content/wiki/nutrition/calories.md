## What are Calories?

Calories are a unit of energy. Everything you eat and drink contains calories (except water).

:::takeaway
**Energy balance determines weight change.** Eat more than you burn = gain weight. Eat less = lose weight. It really is that simple.
:::

## Energy Balance

- **Surplus:** Eat more calories than you burn → weight gain
- **Deficit:** Eat fewer calories than you burn → weight loss
- **Maintenance:** Eat equal to what you burn → weight stable

## How many do you need?

Your daily needs depend on age, sex, weight, and activity level.

:::checklist
- Low activity | 28-30 kcal × bodyweight (kg)
- Active | 32-35 kcal × bodyweight (kg)
- Very active | 38-42 kcal × bodyweight (kg)
:::

## Calorie sources

- **Carbohydrates** - 4 kcal per gram
- **[[protein]]** - 4 kcal per gram
- **Fats** - 9 kcal per gram
- **Alcohol** - 7 kcal per gram

## Common mistakes

- **Extreme deficits** - Crash diets backfire, aim for 300-500 kcal deficit
- **Not tracking** - You can't manage what you don't measure
- **Ignoring protein** - Prioritize [[protein]] when cutting to preserve muscle
