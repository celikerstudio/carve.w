## What is Protein?

Protein is essential for muscle building and recovery. It consists of amino acids—the building blocks of your muscles.

:::takeaway
For muscle growth, aim for **1.6 to 2.2 grams per kg bodyweight** daily. Example: 80kg = 128-176g protein per day.
:::

## How does it work?

Muscle Protein Synthesis (MPS) is triggered when you consume protein. To maximize [[hypertrophy]], you need to exceed Muscle Protein Breakdown (MPB) consistently.

## Best sources

**Animal sources:**
- Chicken, turkey, beef
- Fish (salmon, tuna)
- Eggs and dairy

**Plant sources:**
- Legumes, tofu, tempeh
- Nuts and seeds

## Timing & Distribution

:::checklist
- Breakfast | Start with at least 30g to break the overnight fast.
- Post-Workout | Get protein within 2 hours of training for optimal recovery.
- Pre-Sleep | Casein or slow-digesting protein improves overnight recovery.
:::

## Common mistakes

- **Eating too little** - Most beginners underestimate their needs
- **Uneven distribution** - 10g breakfast + 80g dinner is suboptimal
- **Obsessing over timing** - Total daily intake matters most
