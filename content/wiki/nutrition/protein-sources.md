## What are protein sources?
Protein sources provide amino acids to repair and build muscle. Quality sources offer complete amino acid profiles, good digestibility, and practical portions that fit daily targets.

:::takeaway
Anchor meals with **20-40 g** high-quality protein (meat, fish, eggs, dairy, soy, whey) to reach **1.6-2.2 g/kg/day**.
:::

## How does it work?
Dietary protein supplies essential amino acids, especially leucine, to trigger muscle protein synthesis. Spreading protein across meals keeps synthesis pulses steady. Combining plant sources can make complete profiles. Protein also aids satiety during cuts and supports recovery in surpluses.

## Practical application
Build each meal around a protein anchor: chicken, beef, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, tofu/tempeh, or whey. Mix plant proteins (legumes + grains) for completeness. Distribute 3-5 feedings of 20-40 g. Use shakes to fill gaps, not replace meals. Adjust lean/fatty choices to hit macro targets for bulking or cutting.

:::checklist
- Daily target | Hit **1.6-2.2 g/kg** across **3-5** feedings.
- Meal anchors | Use meat, fish, eggs, dairy, soy, or blended plant proteins.
- Convenience | Keep whey or ready options for busy days; favor whole foods for satiety and micros.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Under-dosing meals (<20 g), missing leucine thresholds.
- Relying only on shakes, skipping micronutrients and satiety from whole foods.
- Not combining plant proteins when needed for completeness.
