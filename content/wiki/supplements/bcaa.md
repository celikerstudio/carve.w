## What are BCAAs?
Branched-chain amino acids (leucine, isoleucine, valine) are essential amino acids often marketed for muscle retention and fatigue reduction.

:::takeaway
If you already hit **1.6-2.2 g/kg** protein, BCAAs add little; use **5-10 g** intra only if training fasted or protein is low.
:::

## How does it work?
Leucine triggers muscle protein synthesis, but full-length protein provides better results due to complete amino profiles. For those with adequate protein, extra BCAAs rarely improve outcomes. They may help reduce perceived fatigue slightly in low-protein or fasted conditions.

## Practical application
Prioritize whole protein (whey, food). Consider 5-10 g BCAAs intra if you must train fasted or cannot eat protein near training. Otherwise, skip and invest in total protein and carbs. Stay hydrated; some BCAA drinks lack electrolytes.

:::checklist
- Protein first | Hit **1.6-2.2 g/kg** total protein before buying BCAAs.
- Use case | Only helpful when fasted/low protein pre-training.
- Dose | **5-10 g** intra if needed.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Replacing real protein with BCAAs and missing total targets.
- Assuming BCAAs boost growth when protein intake is already sufficient.
- Ignoring carbs/electrolytes for long sessions.
