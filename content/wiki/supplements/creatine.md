## What is Creatine?

Creatine is one of the most researched and effective supplements for strength and muscle mass. Your body produces it naturally, but you can also get it through food (meat, fish) or supplements.

:::takeaway
Creatine monohydrate is the **gold standard**—cheap, effective, and backed by decades of research. Other forms (HCL, buffered) aren't proven to be better.
:::

## How does it work?

Creatine increases phosphate reserves in your muscles. This provides:
- More energy during short, intense efforts
- Better performance in strength training
- Faster recovery between sets

## Dosing

:::checklist
- Standard dose | Take 3-5 grams daily. Timing doesn't matter much.
- Loading phase | Optional: 20g/day for 5-7 days speeds up saturation.
- Consistency | Daily use is key—skipping days reduces effectiveness.
:::

## Common mistakes

- **Cycling on/off** - Not necessary, creatine is safe for continuous use
- **Fancy forms** - Monohydrate works best, don't overpay for marketing
- **Underdosing** - 3-5g daily is the minimum effective dose
