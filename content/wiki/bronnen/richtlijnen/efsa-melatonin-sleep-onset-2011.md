> On the basis of the data presented, the Panel concludes that a cause and effect relationship has been established between the consumption of melatonin and reduction of sleep onset latency.

> The Panel considers that in order to obtain the claimed effect, 1 mg of melatonin should be consumed close to bedtime. The target population is the general population.

> The following wording reflects the scientific evidence: “Melatonin helps to reduce the time to fall asleep”.
