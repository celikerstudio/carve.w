## What is a mass gainer?
Mass gainers are high-calorie powders combining carbs, protein, and sometimes fats to simplify calorie surplus intake. They are convenience tools, not magic muscle builders.

:::takeaway
Use gainers only if you cannot hit a **150-300 kcal/day** surplus with food; choose **~20-40 g protein** and moderate sugar options.
:::

## How does it work?
They provide dense calories to meet surplus goals for [[bulking]]. Protein supports muscle repair; carbs supply energy for [[volume]] and [[intensity]]. Whole foods often provide better satiety and micronutrients, but gainers can help high-calorie needs or busy schedules.

## Practical application
Pick products with clear macros (e.g., 20-40 g protein/serving) and reasonable carbs, not excessive sugars. Use smaller servings split through the day to aid digestion. Pair with whole-food meals for micronutrients and fiber. Track total calories to avoid overshooting your surplus.

:::checklist
- Need check | Only add if food intake falls short of surplus.
- Label | Choose balanced macros; avoid extreme sugar loads.
- Portions | Split servings; monitor digestion and total calories.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Relying on gainers instead of improving meals, causing micronutrient gaps.
- Overshooting calories and gaining excess fat.
- Ignoring digestion—huge shakes can cause GI distress.
