## What is a bro split?
A bro split trains one major muscle group per day (e.g., chest day, back day), usually 5 days per week. It concentrates session volume on a single area while lowering per-muscle frequency.

:::takeaway
Use bro splits with **high quality sets** and consider adding a second light touch per muscle later in the week to improve frequency and growth.
:::

## How does it work?
Heavy same-day volume can drive [[hypertrophy]] if sets are near failure, but low frequency (1x/week) may limit practice and protein synthesis pulses. Adding a lighter second hit or rotating emphasis can improve results. Bro splits can be joint-friendly when fatigue per session is managed.

## Practical application
Run 4-6 exercises for the target muscle, 3-4 sets each, 6-15 reps, keeping 1-3 RIR. To boost frequency, add a few light sets for that muscle on another day. Prioritize compounds early, then isolations. Monitor weekly set totals to stay near 10-20 per muscle. Deload every 6-10 weeks or when soreness lingers and performance stalls.

:::checklist
- Volume | Keep **10-20 sets/muscle/week**, even if split across one or two days.
- Frequency | Add a light secondary touch for lagging muscles to reach ~**2x/week**.
- Effort | Most sets **1-3 RIR**; avoid turning every session into all-out failure.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Hammering one day to exhaustion with poor-quality later sets.
- Relying on 1x/week frequency without secondary touches, slowing progress.
- Ignoring recovery and sleep needs after very high single-day volumes.
