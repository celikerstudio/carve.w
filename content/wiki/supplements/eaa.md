## What are EAAs?
Essential amino acids (EAAs) include the nine amino acids the body cannot produce. They can stimulate muscle protein synthesis when full protein meals are not available.

:::takeaway
EAAs can bridge gaps when you cannot eat protein; **10-15 g** can sub in, but whole protein is preferred.
:::

## How does it work?
EAAs provide the full essential profile, including leucine, to trigger synthesis. However, they lack the satiety and completeness of whole proteins. For adequate protein eaters, EAAs add little; they are a convenience tool when real food or shakes are impossible.

## Practical application
Use 10-15 g EAAs pre/intra if you cannot get protein for several hours around training. Otherwise, rely on meals or [[whey-protein]]. Check products for flavoring and sweeteners if sensitive. Prioritize hydration and carbs for performance.

:::checklist
- Preference | Choose food/whey first; EAAs are a backup.
- Dose | **10-15 g** when needed around training.
- Context | Useful during travel/fasted windows; unnecessary when protein is covered.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Treating EAAs as superior to whole protein.
- Double-spending on EAAs when protein intake is already high.
- Ignoring carbs/electrolytes that more directly support performance.
