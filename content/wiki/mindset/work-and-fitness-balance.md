## What is work and fitness balance?
Work and fitness balance is structuring training, nutrition, and recovery around job demands. It keeps consistency high despite a busy schedule.

:::takeaway
Lock **3-5 weekly sessions**, prep simple meals, and anchor workouts to fixed times to reduce friction on busy days.
:::

## How does it work?
Time-blocking, defaults, and environment design cut decision fatigue. Short, focused sessions maintain [[progressive-overload]] when time is tight. Meal prep and protein-rich snacks protect macros. Sleep scheduling sustains energy and adherence.

## Practical application
Pick set training days/times (e.g., lunch or post-work). Use minimum viable sessions (two compounds + accessories) when slammed. Prep 2-3 go-to meals that hit [[protein]] and calories. Keep a gym bag packed and calendar reminders on. If travel, use hotel DB/bodyweight templates. Prioritize 7-9 hours of sleep with consistent wake times.

:::checklist
- Schedule | Fixed **3-5x/week** slots; use shorter sessions on hectic days.
- Food | Prep protein-centric meals/snacks; keep a simple grocery list.
- Sleep | Protect 7-9h; align caffeine cut-off to bed time.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Skipping entirely on busy weeks instead of scaling down.
- Overcomplicating meals, leading to takeout reliance.
- Letting sleep slide, tanking training quality.
