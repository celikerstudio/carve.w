## What is melatonin?
Melatonin is a hormone regulating sleep-wake cycles. Supplements can aid sleep onset, especially for jet lag or shift work.

:::takeaway
Use **0.5-3 mg** 30-60 minutes pre-bed for onset; prioritize sleep hygiene first.
:::

## How does it work?
Melatonin signals the body to wind down. Low doses often work as well as high; excess can cause grogginess. It helps shift circadian timing but cannot replace good [[sleep-hygiene]].

## Practical application
Take 0.5-3 mg 30-60 minutes before bed. Keep lights dim in the evening; get morning light. Avoid high doses unless directed. If ineffective after improving hygiene, reassess needs with a clinician. Do not combine with driving or machinery soon after dosing.

:::checklist
- Dose | Start **0.5-1 mg**; only increase if needed.
- Timing | **30-60 min** pre-bed; keep evening light low.
- Context | Use for jet lag/shift work; keep hygiene habits strong.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Megadosing, causing grogginess or vivid dreams.
- Using melatonin to mask poor routines (late screens, caffeine).
- Taking and staying on bright devices, negating its effect.
