## What are rest days?
Rest days are planned days without intense training to allow recovery, adaptation, and injury prevention. They are part of effective programming.

:::takeaway
Include **1-3 rest days/week** depending on training load; prioritize [[sleep]], nutrition, and light movement.
:::

## How does it work?
Rest reduces accumulated fatigue from [[volume]] and [[intensity]], allowing supercompensation. Muscle repair, nervous system recovery, and connective tissue resilience all improve when stress is cycled with recovery.

## Practical application
Plan rest after heavy or high-volume days. Keep steps/light movement, but avoid strenuous work. Eat to match goals (deficit/surplus) and maintain protein. Use the day to prep meals, sleep more, and review technique. If extremely fatigued, consider extra rest or a deload.

:::checklist
- Frequency | **1-3** per week based on load and recovery.
- Activity | Light walks/mobility only; avoid hard training.
- Support | Hit protein, hydrate, and sleep extra if needed.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Treating rest as inactivity and under-eating, slowing recovery.
- Ignoring signals and skipping rest, leading to overuse issues.
- Filling rest days with hard recreational efforts that add fatigue.
