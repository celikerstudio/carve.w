## What is pre-workout nutrition?
Pre-workout nutrition fuels training by providing carbs for energy and protein for muscle repair signals. It aims to improve performance and reduce perceived effort.

:::takeaway
Eat **20-60 g carbs** and **20-40 g protein** **1-3 hours** before lifting; keep fats/fiber moderate for easy digestion.
:::

## How does it work?
Carbs raise blood glucose and spare glycogen, supporting [[volume]] and [[intensity]]. Protein elevates amino acids, priming muscle protein synthesis when training-induced signals hit. Moderate fat/fiber keeps the meal satiating without slowing digestion too much.

## Practical application
Options: rice/chicken, oats/whey/fruit, yogurt/fruit/granola. If close to training (<60 minutes), use faster carbs (fruit, white rice) and lighter protein (whey). Hydrate and add electrolytes if hot/long. Keep caffeine timing sensible to protect [[sleep]]. Adjust portion size to your session length and gut comfort.

:::checklist
- Timing | **1-3 hours** pre-lift; closer = lighter, faster-digesting.
- Composition | **20-60 g carbs**, **20-40 g protein**, low-moderate fat/fiber.
- Hydration | Start hydrated; add sodium if sweating heavily.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Training on empty when sessions are long/hard, dropping performance.
- Using heavy, fatty meals right before lifting, causing GI distress.
- Skipping protein entirely, missing an easy anabolic window.
