## What is goal setting?
Goal setting defines clear, actionable targets for training and nutrition. It improves focus, adherence, and progress tracking.

:::takeaway
Use **SMART goals**, tie them to weekly actions, and review every **2-4 weeks**.
:::

## How does it work?
Specific goals create direction; measurable metrics enable feedback; realistic timelines improve motivation. Linking goals to behaviors (sessions, protein targets, steps) bridges intention and action. Regular reviews keep plans aligned with life and recovery.

## Practical application
Set 1-3 main goals (e.g., add 5 kg to squat in 8 weeks, lose 0.5 kg/week). Break into weekly actions: 3-5 lifts/week, **1.6-2.2 g/kg** [[protein]], set steps. Track lifts and weight; adjust after 2-4 weeks based on trends. Write goals down and place reminders where you see them.

:::checklist
- Define | **SMART** goals with clear numbers and timelines.
- Behaviors | Map to weekly actions (sessions, protein, steps, sleep).
- Review | Check progress **every 2-4 weeks** and adjust calmly.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Vague goals without metrics or timelines.
- Setting too many goals, scattering effort.
- Not reviewing data, leading to random program changes.
