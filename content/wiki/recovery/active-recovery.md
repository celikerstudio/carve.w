## What is active recovery?
Active recovery uses low-intensity movement to promote blood flow and reduce stiffness without adding significant fatigue. It helps you feel and move better between hard sessions.

:::takeaway
Use **10-30 minutes** of easy movement (walk, cycle, mobility) on rest days or after training to aid circulation and recovery.
:::

## How does it work?
Light activity increases circulation, helping clear metabolites and deliver nutrients without taxing the nervous system. It can reduce perceived soreness and maintain movement quality for upcoming [[volume]] and [[intensity]] sessions.

## Practical application
Walks, light cycling, mobility flows, or swimming at easy pace. Keep intensity low (you can nose-breathe and talk). 10-30 minutes is enough. Pair with hydration and [[sleep]]. Avoid turning active recovery into another workout.

:::checklist
- Intensity | Keep it easy; nasal breathing, conversational pace.
- Duration | **10-30 min** sessions on rest or post-lift days.
- Pairing | Combine with hydration and gentle mobility.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Going too hard and adding hidden fatigue.
- Skipping movement entirely on rest days, staying stiff and sluggish.
- Using “recovery” sessions to cram more junk volume.
