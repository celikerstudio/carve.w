## What is Hypertrophy?

Hypertrophy means muscle growth—the enlargement of your muscle fibers. This is what most people in the gym are training for.

:::takeaway
The three pillars of hypertrophy: **training stimulus**, **adequate [[protein]]**, and **sufficient recovery**. Miss one, and progress stalls.
:::

## How does it work?

1. **Training** causes microscopic damage to muscle fibers
2. **Recovery** + nutrition repairs and strengthens the fibers
3. **Repetition** over time = larger muscles

## Optimal training for hypertrophy

:::checklist
- Volume | 10-20 sets per muscle group per week.
- Intensity | 60-80% of 1RM (6-15 reps per set).
- Frequency | Train each muscle 2x per week minimum.
- Progression | Apply [[progressive-overload]] consistently.
:::

## Hypertrophy vs Strength

- **Hypertrophy** - 6-15 reps, 1-2 min rest
- **Strength** - 1-5 reps, 3-5 min rest

You can combine both in your program.

## Common mistakes

- **Not enough volume** - Most beginners do too little, not too much
- **Neglecting nutrition** - You can't build muscle in a caloric deficit
- **Impatience** - Visible results take 3-6 months minimum
