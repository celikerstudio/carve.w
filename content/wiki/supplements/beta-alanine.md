## What is beta-alanine?
Beta-alanine is an amino acid that raises muscle carnosine, buffering acidity in high-rep or interval work. It can improve performance in efforts lasting 30 seconds to several minutes.

:::takeaway
Take **3.2-6.4 g/day** (split doses) for **4+ weeks** to build carnosine; tingling (paresthesia) is normal and harmless.
:::

## How does it work?
By increasing carnosine, beta-alanine reduces hydrogen ion buildup, delaying fatigue in moderate-duration efforts. It’s most relevant for high-rep lifting density, intervals, and some sports. It does little for short max strength or very long endurance.

## Practical application
Split doses (e.g., 1.6 g twice daily) to reduce tingles. Consistency matters more than timing; take with meals if preferred. Pair with adequate [[creatine]] and carbs for broader performance support. If tingling is bothersome, use sustained-release products or smaller doses.

:::checklist
- Dose | **3.2-6.4 g/day** split; load for **4+ weeks**.
- Timing | Any time; consistency over timing.
- Use case | Best for high-rep density/intervals, less for pure 1RM strength.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Expecting immediate effects; it needs weeks of dosing.
- Using it to replace programming, carbs, or rest.
- Stopping early, before carnosine builds.
