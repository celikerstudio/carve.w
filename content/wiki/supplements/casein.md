## What is casein?
Casein is a slow-digesting milk protein that provides a steady release of amino acids. It helps meet daily protein targets and can support overnight muscle repair.

:::takeaway
Use **20-40 g** casein to help reach **1.6-2.2 g/kg** daily protein; great before bed for slow release.
:::

## How does it work?
Casein forms a gel in the stomach, slowing digestion and prolonging amino acid availability. This can sustain muscle protein synthesis over longer windows, complementing faster proteins like [[whey-protein]]. It aids satiety in cuts and supports recovery in all phases.

## Practical application
Take 20-40 g casein as a shake or use cottage cheese/Greek yogurt at night. Fit it into total protein goals rather than adding excess. If lactose sensitive, choose micellar casein with low lactose or test tolerance. Combine with carbs post-lift if using it then, though whey is often preferred immediately post.

:::checklist
- Dose | **20-40 g** servings; count toward daily total.
- Timing | Useful pre-bed for slow release; also fine daytime if preferred.
- Tolerance | Choose low-lactose options if sensitive.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Treating casein as mandatory instead of one option to hit protein targets.
- Ignoring total daily protein while focusing on timing.
- Overeating casein on top of already sufficient protein.
