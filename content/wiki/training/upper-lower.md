## What is an upper lower split?
An upper/lower split alternates sessions for upper- and lower-body lifts, typically 4 days per week. It balances frequency, recovery, and session length for both strength and size goals.

:::takeaway
Train **4x/week** (ULUL), target **10-20 sets/muscle/week**, and keep compounds at **1-3 RIR** for quality progress.
:::

## How does it work?
Each muscle gets 2 sessions weekly, allowing higher per-session focus than full body but less overlap than bro splits. Spreading compounds across four days reduces session fatigue and supports stronger lifts. Recovery hinges on sleep, nutrition, and managing high-load days to avoid joint overload.

## Practical application
Structure days: Upper (horizontal press/row focus) / Lower (squat/hinge) / off / Upper (vertical press/pull) / Lower (hinge/squat). Use 2-4 compounds plus accessories per day. Track sets per pattern and keep within 10-20 per muscle weekly. Deload every 6-10 weeks or sooner if performance dips. If recovery is tight, cap sessions at ~75-90 minutes and limit failure work.

:::checklist
- Weekly map | Run **ULUL** with at least **1 rest day** spaced in.
- Volume | Maintain **10-20 sets/muscle/week**; spread heavy lifts across days.
- Effort | Keep most compounds at **1-3 RIR**; deload before fatigue piles up.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Overstuffing sessions with too many compounds, leading to rushed form.
- Neglecting rest spacing, creating back-to-back heavy hinge/squat days.
- Never deloading, letting shoulder/hip fatigue accumulate.
