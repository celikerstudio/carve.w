## What is cutting?
Cutting is a planned calorie deficit to reduce body fat while preserving muscle. It relies on adequate protein, resistance training, and manageable deficit sizing to keep performance and adherence high.

:::takeaway
Aim to lose **0.5-1.0% body weight/week** with **1.6-2.2 g/kg** [[protein]], a **300-600 kcal/day** deficit, and steady lifting.
:::

## How does it work?
Eating below maintenance forces the body to mobilize stored fat. Protein and lifting protect lean mass and strength. Moderate deficits maintain [[sleep]], training quality, and adherence better than aggressive cuts. Tracking trends prevents overreacting to daily weight noise.

## Practical application
Estimate maintenance, subtract 300-600 kcal. Set protein 1.6-2.2 g/kg, fats ~0.6-0.9 g/kg, rest carbs to fuel [[volume]] and [[intensity]]. Use weekly weight averages and performance to adjust by 100-200 kcal if stalled 2+ weeks. Prioritize fiber (10-15 g/1,000 kcal), hydration, and meal timing that tames hunger. Keep steps consistent to avoid hidden activity drops.

:::checklist
- Deficit size | **300-600 kcal/day** for **0.5-1.0%/week** loss.
- Muscle retention | Lift 2-4x/week; hold **1.6-2.2 g/kg** protein.
- Hunger control | Use fiber, hydration, and stable meal timing.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Cutting too hard, tanking performance and recovery.
- Slashing carbs so low that training quality dives.
- Reacting to single weigh-ins instead of weekly trends.
