## What is magnesium?
Magnesium is a mineral involved in muscle contraction, nerve function, and energy metabolism. Many diets fall short, and low magnesium can affect sleep and recovery.

:::takeaway
Target **300-400 mg/day** from food plus supplements if needed; take glycinate/taurate forms if sensitive.
:::

## How does it work?
Magnesium acts as a cofactor in hundreds of reactions, supporting ATP use, muscle relaxation, and nervous system balance. Low levels can impair performance and [[sleep]]. Supplements may aid sleep quality in deficient individuals.

## Practical application
Eat nuts, seeds, legumes, whole grains, and greens. If supplementing, use 200-400 mg elemental magnesium, preferably glycinate or taurate to reduce GI upset; avoid high-dose oxide. Take in the evening if it helps relaxation. Check interactions with medications.

:::checklist
- Intake | **300-400 mg/day** total; food first, supplement if needed.
- Form | Choose glycinate/taurate for better tolerance.
- Timing | Evening dosing may aid relaxation; monitor GI response.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Using high-dose oxide, causing GI issues.
- Assuming it fixes sleep without addressing hygiene and [[stress-management]].
- Overdosing; more is not always better.
