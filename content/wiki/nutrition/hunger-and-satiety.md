## What are hunger and satiety strategies?
Managing hunger and satiety helps you stick to calorie goals. Food choice, timing, and habits can curb overeating and improve comfort in both cuts and maintenance.

:::takeaway
Use **protein at each meal**, **fiber 10-15 g/1,000 kcal**, and fluids; bias larger meals around training for adherence.
:::

## How does it work?
Protein and fiber slow digestion and improve fullness. Higher food volume (fruits, vegetables, potatoes) increases stretch signals. Regular meals steady blood sugar. Adequate [[sleep]] and stress control reduce hunger hormones. Liquids and ultra-processed foods often under-satiate per calorie.

## Practical application
Anchor each meal with 20-40 g protein and high-volume carbs/veggies. Choose whole foods over calorie-dense snacks. Distribute calories to when hunger is highest (often after training). Drink water throughout the day. Limit liquid calories and alcohol. If cutting, pre-log meals to prevent “calorie drift.”

:::checklist
- Meal build | **20-40 g protein** + high-volume carbs/veg per meal.
- Fiber & fluids | **10-15 g/1,000 kcal** fiber; sip water steadily.
- Timing | Place bigger meals when hunger peaks; avoid liquid calories.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Low protein and fiber, leading to constant hunger.
- Relying on liquid calories that don’t satisfy.
- Poor sleep/stress driving cravings and overeating.
