## What is fitness 40+?
Training after 40 follows the same principles but with extra attention to recovery, joint care, and muscle retention. Strength and muscle gains remain achievable with smart programming.

:::takeaway
Lift **2-4x/week**, keep **1-3 RIR**, sleep **7-9h**, and prioritize protein (**1.6-2.2 g/kg**) and joint-friendly exercise selection.
:::

## How does it work?
Aging can slow recovery and reduce anabolic sensitivity. Higher protein, consistent lifting, and managing [[volume]]/[[intensity]] sustain muscle and bone health. Joint-friendly variations and longer warm-ups help maintain training quality.

## Practical application
Use a mix of compounds and machines; adjust grips/stances for comfort. Keep most sets 1-3 RIR; deload every 4-8 weeks. Warm up thoroughly and include balance/mobility work. Ensure protein and possibly creatine; monitor sleep/stress. Address niggles early with load tweaks and technique review.

:::checklist
- Programming | **2-4** lifting days; controlled effort; regular deloads.
- Recovery | Sleep **7-9h**, manage stress, and fuel appropriately.
- Joint care | Use joint-friendly variations; include mobility/balance.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Training to failure constantly, outrunning recovery.
- Ignoring joint feedback and forcing painful variations.
- Under-eating protein, slowing muscle retention.
