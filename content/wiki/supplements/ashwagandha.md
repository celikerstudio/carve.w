## What is ashwagandha?
Ashwagandha is an adaptogenic herb studied for stress reduction, sleep support, and potential performance benefits.

:::takeaway
Common dose **300-600 mg/day** of KSM-66 or Sensoril extract; may lower stress and modestly aid strength in some studies.
:::

## How does it work?
Ashwagandha may reduce cortisol and improve perceived stress/sleep, indirectly supporting training and recovery. Some small studies show strength/size benefits, but effects are modest and variable. Quality extracts matter for consistency.

## Practical application
Use 300-600 mg/day of a standardized extract with meals. Track sleep, mood, and training to see if it helps. Do not use in pregnancy; consult if on medication. Pair with foundational habits: [[sleep]], nutrition, and load management.

:::checklist
- Dose | **300-600 mg/day** standardized extract.
- Safety | Avoid if pregnant; check meds with a clinician.
- Tracking | Log sleep/stress/performance to judge effect.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Expecting large muscle gains; effects are mild.
- Using non-standardized products with unknown potency.
- Replacing stress management basics with a supplement.
