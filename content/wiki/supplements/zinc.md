## What is zinc?
Zinc is a mineral important for immunity, hormone production, and recovery. Deficiency can impair performance and healing.

:::takeaway
Get **8-11 mg/day** from food; supplement **15-30 mg** only if intake is low or labs suggest deficiency.
:::

## How does it work?
Zinc supports enzyme function, protein synthesis, and immune health. Low zinc can affect testosterone and recovery. Most omnivores meet needs with a varied diet; high-dose supplements are rarely necessary and can disrupt copper balance.

## Practical application
Eat meat, shellfish, dairy, nuts, and beans (soak/ferment to improve absorption). If supplementing, keep to 15-30 mg/day and pair with copper if using higher doses longer term. Take with food to avoid nausea. Test levels if concerned.

:::checklist
- Food first | Meat/shellfish/dairy/nuts/beans for zinc.
- Dose | Supplement **15-30 mg** only if needed; avoid chronic high dosing.
- Balance | Watch copper if using higher zinc long term.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Taking high-dose zinc daily without deficiency evidence.
- Ignoring copper balance, leading to deficiency.
- Using zinc to chase hormone boosts instead of fixing diet/sleep.
