## What is foam rolling?
Foam rolling is self-myofascial release using a roller to reduce stiffness and improve short-term mobility. It can ease soreness perception and prep tissues for movement.

:::takeaway
Roll tight areas for **30-90 seconds** each, then move through the range you plan to train.
:::

## How does it work?
Rolling applies pressure that may reduce neural tone and improve tolerance to stretch, briefly increasing range of motion. It can decrease perceived soreness, making movement more comfortable. Effects are transient; follow with active movement.

## Practical application
Before lifting, spend 5-10 minutes on key spots (quads, glutes, lats), 30-90s each. After rolling, do dynamic drills for the same areas. Don’t press so hard that you bruise. Use rolling to complement, not replace, proper warm-ups and strength through range.

:::checklist
- Dose | **30-90s** per muscle; 5-10 minutes total.
- Sequence | Roll, then dynamic move through that range.
- Pressure | Moderate, not crippling; avoid bone/joints.
:::

## Common mistakes
- Over-rolling with excessive pressure, causing bruising.
- Relying on rolling instead of fixing technique and load management.
- Skipping active movement after rolling, losing the temporary ROM gains.
