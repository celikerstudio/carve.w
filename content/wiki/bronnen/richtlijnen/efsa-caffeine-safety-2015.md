## Passages (handmatig geciteerd)

Letterlijk overgenomen uit de PDF van https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2015.4102 (EFSA Journal 2015;13(5):4102, open access maar CC BY-ND, dus alleen deze zinnen) op 2026-10-21. Gelezen in de Abstract van de volledige tekst.

> Single doses of caffeine up to 200 mg (about 3 mg/kg bw for a 70-kg adult) do not give rise to safety concerns.

> Habitual caffeine consumption up to 400 mg per day does not give rise to safety concerns for non-pregnant adults.

> Habitual caffeine consumption up to 200 mg per day by pregnant women does not give rise to safety concerns for the fetus.

Aanvullend, letterlijk van de EFSA-pagina https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/topics/topic/caffeine (gelezen op 2026-10-21):

(Onder de kop "Pregnant/lactating women":)

> Caffeine intakes from all sources up to 200mg per day consumed throughout the day do not raise safety concerns for the foetus.

(Onder de kop "Children and adolescents":)

> A safety level of 3mg/kg bw per day is also proposed for habitual caffeine consumption by children and adolescents.

Let op: de zin over 200 mg per dag staat op de EFSA-pagina onder de kop "Pregnant/lactating women", maar de zin zelf noemt alleen de foetus. Een zin die expliciet borstvoeding noemt heb ik op de pagina niet gevonden. De pagina noemt "pregnant and lactating women" wel als groep die EFSA beoordeelde: Die zin staat wel in de samenvatting van de opinion zelf, zie onderaan.

> in specific groups such as children, adolescents, adults, the elderly, pregnant and lactating women, and people performing physical exercise
